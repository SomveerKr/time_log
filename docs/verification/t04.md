DST handling uses the earlier valid offset for repeats and the transition's
next valid local time for gaps, as defined by the
[Java ZoneRules API](https://docs.oracle.com/en/java/javase/17/docs/api/java.base/java/time/zone/ZoneRules.html)
and [ZoneOffsetTransition](https://docs.oracle.com/en/java/javase/17/docs/api/java.base/java/time/zone/ZoneOffsetTransition.html).

Reminder delivery remains T05, and the UI states it is unavailable in this
increment. SPEC's strict window-end cutoff is currently implemented: a final
delivery even 1 ms after the end is discarded. Clarification about allowing
the final minute is pending; no policy change has been assumed. Real delivery,
permission/Doze/reboot behavior and physical reliability are untested here.
